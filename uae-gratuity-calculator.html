How UAE gratuity is calculated

Daily basic wage = basic monthly salary ÷ 30. Gratuity is based on basic salary only, not allowances such as housing or transport.

If you have completed at least one year of continuous service, you get 21 days of basic wage for each of the first five years and 30 days for each year after that. Part years are counted in proportion to the time worked. The total cannot be more than two years' wage.

Example: AED 8,000 basic for 7 years. Daily wage = AED 266.67. Years 1–5: 5 × 21 × 266.67 = AED 28,000. Years 6–7: 2 × 30 × 266.67 = AED 16,000. Total ≈ AED 44,000.

Under the current law, resignation and termination are generally treated the same, and the employer should pay within 14 days of the contract ending.

This is an estimate for private-sector employees under the UAE Labour Law. Free zones such as DIFC and ADGM, government jobs, unpaid leave and your contract terms can change the result. For an official figure, check with your employer or MOHRE.

Frequently asked questions

How is gratuity calculated in the UAE?

Divide your basic monthly salary by 30 to get the daily wage. You receive 21 days of that wage for each of the first five years of service and 30 days for each year after that, capped at two years' wage.

Do I get gratuity if I resign?

Under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, employees who resign after at least one year of continuous service are generally entitled to the same gratuity as those whose contract is ended by the employer.

Do I get gratuity for less than one year?

No. Gratuity applies only after completing at least one year of continuous service.

Is gratuity calculated on basic or total salary?

On basic salary only. Allowances such as housing, transport and phone are not included.

When must my employer pay gratuity?

Wages and end-of-service benefits should be paid within 14 days of the contract ending.
