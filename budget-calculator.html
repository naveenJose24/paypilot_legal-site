How the calculation works

Safe to spend = salary − fixed bills − savings goal − already spent. Divide that by the days left until payday to get a daily limit. It is a better number than your bank balance, because your balance still includes rent, EMIs and savings that are already spoken for.

The 50/30/20 rule shows a reference split of your salary: about half for needs, 30% for wants and 20% for savings. If your fixed bills are above the 50% line, the calculator flags it, which is common with high rents in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 50/30/20 budget rule?

Put about 50% of take-home pay toward needs such as rent, utilities and loan EMIs, 30% toward wants, and 20% toward savings and extra debt repayment. It is a starting point, not a strict rule.

How much is safe to spend per day?

Take your salary, subtract fixed bills and your savings goal, subtract what you have already spent this month, then divide what is left by the days remaining until payday.

Does this work for a Dubai or UAE salary?

Yes. Choose AED, enter your monthly take-home salary and include rent, DEWA, school fees, car or loan EMIs and remittances you send home as fixed commitments.

Is my data saved or sent anywhere?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser and nothing you type is stored or sent.
