How EMI is calculated

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of monthly instalments.

Example: AED 100,000 at 6.5% for 48 months gives an EMI of about AED 2,371, total interest of about AED 13,830 and total repayment of about AED 113,830.

This calculator uses the reducing-balance method that most banks use. Some UAE personal loans are quoted as a flat rate, which costs more than the same reducing rate. Ask your bank for the reducing (APR) rate to compare fairly. Processing fees and insurance are not included.

Frequently asked questions

What is EMI?

EMI (equated monthly instalment) is the fixed amount you repay every month on a loan. It covers part of the principal and the interest.

How do I calculate EMI?

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of months.

What is the difference between flat and reducing interest?

A flat rate charges interest on the original loan amount for the whole tenure. A reducing rate charges interest only on the balance still owed, so the same headline rate costs less on a reducing basis.

Does a longer tenure reduce the cost?

No. A longer tenure lowers the monthly EMI but increases the total interest you pay.
