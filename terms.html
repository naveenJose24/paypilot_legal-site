4. Auto-capture

Auto-capture can log payments from bank SMS, card payments, and bank or UPI app notifications. It is optional and depends on things outside our control:

  • On iPhone, you create Shortcuts automations yourself, following our guide. On Android, you grant notification access.
  • Your device's operating system, which may change, delay, or stop these features.
  • The wording your bank uses. PayPilot reads common formats from banks in India and the UAE, but it may miss messages, misread an amount or merchant, or skip unusual formats.

To protect you, PayPilot ignores some messages on purpose, such as texts from phone numbers or email addresses, promotions, and likely scams. Money received into an account it does not recognise waits for your confirmation. Auto-capture is provided on a best-effort basis. Check captured entries, and edit or delete them as needed. PayPilot never moves money, opens links, or contacts anyone on your behalf. Never send money back to someone because of a message alone.
