Track expenses from bank SMS on iPhone

Android apps can read payment notifications, but iPhone apps can't read your messages. PayPilot works around this the Apple-approved way: a one-time Shortcuts automation sends bank texts to PayPilot, which logs the expense on your phone. Paste a bank SMS below to see what it picks up.

Why iPhone needs a different approach

iOS doesn’t let any app read your SMS inbox, which is good for privacy. Most expense trackers that “read SMS” only work on Android. On iPhone, the only built-in way to act on an incoming text is a Shortcuts personal automation, and PayPilot is built around it.

How it works with PayPilot

  1. Set up once (about 2 minutes). In the Shortcuts app, create an automation: “When I receive a message containing ited”. That catches debited, credited and deposited.
  2. Add PayPilot’s action. Choose “Log bank message”, pass the message content and sender, and turn off “Confirm Before Run”.
  3. Pay as usual. When your bank texts you, PayPilot reads the amount, merchant and card on your phone and logs the expense. Other texts that happen to contain “ited” are dropped.

Add a second automation for Apple Pay taps using the Wallet trigger. The step-by-step auto-capture guide has screenshots for both. You need iOS 17 or later.

Works with banks in India and the UAE

PayPilot reads UPI, debit card, credit card and bank transfer alerts in INR and AED. It is built for alerts from banks such as SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis and Kotak in India, and Emirates NBD, ADCB, FAB and Mashreq in the UAE. If your bank uses a different word, like “spent” or “debit:”, add one more automation with that word.

What stays private

  • iOS only passes the texts your automation matches. PayPilot can’t browse your messages.
  • Messages are read on your iPhone and never uploaded to a server or AI. One-time passwords are discarded straight away.
  • Only the expense is kept: amount, merchant, date and the card’s last 4 digits.
  • No bank login, no account linking.

On Android, PayPilot uses notification access instead, with no Shortcuts needed. Get PayPilot on the App Store.

Frequently asked questions

Can an iPhone app read my bank SMS?

No. iOS doesn't let apps read the SMS inbox. PayPilot uses a Shortcuts automation that you set up once, which passes only matching bank texts to PayPilot.

Does it work automatically after setup?

Yes. With Confirm Before Run turned off, the automation runs in the background whenever a matching bank text arrives, and the expense appears in PayPilot.

Which banks are supported?

PayPilot reads common alert formats from banks in India (UPI, cards, transfers in INR) and the UAE (AED). If your bank's alert uses a word other than debited or credited, add one more automation with that word.

Is my SMS data uploaded anywhere?

No. Messages are read on your iPhone and discarded. Only the expense details are kept, and one-time passwords are thrown away immediately.

Do I need to link my bank account?

No. PayPilot works from SMS alerts and Apple Pay, so there is no bank login or account linking.
